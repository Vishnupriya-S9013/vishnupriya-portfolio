Velora

A warm and welcoming e-commerce experience designed to make discovering baby-care essentials simple & effortless.

Case Study 03

Overview

Velora is a fictional baby-care brand created as a personal design project for my UI/UX portfolio. The project focuses on designing an e-commerce homepage that helps parents discover baby-care products through a simple and welcoming shopping experience.

Year:
2026
Tool:

Project Goals

The goal was to create a warm and trustworthy brand experience while making baby-care products easy to discover. I wanted to design a simple, enjoyable homepage that presents products attractively and allows visitors to explore the brand’s offerings with ease. This project also gave me an opportunity to practise my web UI design skills.

Homepage structure & Design sections

Building the homepage

Decided the section order while designing, organizing the homepage around product discovery, featured products, and brand information.

Key sections

  • Hero Banner — Introduces the brand.
  • Shop by Category — Helps visitors explore product types.
  • Best Sellers — Highlights featured products.
  • Why Parents Choose Us — Presents parent-focused benefits.
  • Parenting Tips — Adds baby-care content.
  • Our Promise & Newsletter — Completes the homepage with brand messaging and subscription options.
